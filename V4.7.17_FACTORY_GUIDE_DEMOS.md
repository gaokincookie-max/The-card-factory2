# v4.7.17 – Animated factory guide prototype

The facility reference pages now use a miniature factory demonstration for resource miners and the smelter. Other facility types retain their existing diagram for now.

- Resource collection: show the 2×2 resource zone, place a miner, lay outgoing belts, then display a moving resource.
- Smelter: place a 1×1 furnace, connect left-output/right-input belts, show an incoming material and an outgoing finished product.
- The miniature is presentation-only and never modifies the factory simulation.
- Play/pause, previous/next step, and restart controls are included.
- Tiles share the live board's `.cell`, `.zone`, `.occupied`, `.belt` classes; size and facility labels come from the facility catalog.
- The same guide is used from the database and the in-factory ⓘ control.

This is an interaction and visual prototype, not a video capture or an actual simulated factory.
